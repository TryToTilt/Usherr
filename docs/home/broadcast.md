# Broadcast and the remote

**Broadcast** is Usherr's look from now on. It's dark and clear, made to be
used from the sofa with a TV remote as well as with a mouse, a keyboard or a
finger. Things stand out by being lighter, not by shadows, and one blue shows
where you are. It has its own font (Inter) and numbers that line up for times.

A browser that never picked a look gets Broadcast. If you picked **Usherr
Neu**, **Neu, new layout** or **Usherr Classic** before, you keep it: those
looks, their menus, their pages and their settings are exactly as they were.
Everything on this page is Broadcast's, except signing in on a TV and
**Finish on your phone**, which work in every look.

The other pages of this guide give the Broadcast way to a page first and the
Classic and Neu way second, where the two differ.

## The one menu

Broadcast has one menu with seven places. It's the same on a computer, a TV
and a phone.

| Place | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Home** | What you were watching, with **Resume** ready to press; **Tonight** (the next showtime, a crew vote, a new manga volume, a game today and, for administrators, when the Server needs you; each card only when it has something to say); **On now** (Live TV channels with what's airing; OK tunes in); Continue, Recently added and For you. |
| **Find** | The search and Ask bar, **Discover** by kind of media (Manga's Discover too) and **Complete your library**, **Roulette**, **Tonight**, people you follow, searching releases (administrators), and **Things you can do**: type what you want to do and get **Show me how** for it. |
| **Library** | The kinds of media, in one order everywhere: Movies, TV shows, Anime, Music, Books (ebooks and audiobooks together), Manga, Comics. Then Sports, **Coming up** (the calendar, Wanted, Following, Leaving soon) and, for administrators, the streamed library. **NSFW 18+** has a line of its own, only for the accounts allowed it. |
| **Live TV** | The guide, what's on now, your channels, Real TV and reminders; the channel editor for administrators. |
| **Movie night** | **Showtimes** and the lobby, **Crews and votes**, **Sports tonight**, and the **Marquee screen** (opens in a new tab). |
| **You** | Taste and votes, **Diary**, **Your year**, **Ticket stubs** and badges, **Your settings**, the **User guide** and **Show me how**. |
| **Server** | For administrators, with a count of what needs you: **Today**, **Activity** and the settings in seven groups (below). |

Library, Movie night, You and Server fold open while you're on one of their
pages. At the bottom of the menu: the players menu, this guide and light or
dark.

**The top bar** is leaner than before: the search and Ask bar, **Show me
how** and your profile. Roulette and Tonight are on **Find**; the next
showtime is on **Home** and **Movie night**.

**On a phone** there's a bar along the bottom: Home, Find, Library, Live TV,
Movie night and You. Server is reached from **You**.

### Menu modes

Under **You › Your settings › Look and language › The menu**, pick how the
menu sits on a computer or a TV:

- **Icons** (the default): a narrow row of icons that opens when you point at
  it, or when you move to it with the remote.
- **Always open**: the whole menu stays in sight.
- **Hidden**: out of the way until the menu button or the left edge of the
  screen opens it.

**With a mouse, the menu slides away** (the row below it) still sets how long
an open menu waits after the pointer leaves. Both are kept in that browser.

## Using a TV remote

The remote works on a TV's own browser (NVIDIA Shield, Google TV, Fire TV and
the like) from the start. On a computer the arrow keys of a keyboard do the
same. A mouse, a finger and typing are never taken over: the moment you use
the mouse, Usherr draws focus the usual way again.

One thing always has the focus, and it's always easy to see: it's filled
with the blue, with white text.

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Arrows** | Move to the nearest thing that way: Up and Down between rows, Left and Right along a row. Left at the left edge of a page moves into the menu (in **Icons** it opens as it gets the focus). |
| **OK** (Enter) | Presses what has the focus. |
| **Back** (the remote's Back, Backspace) | Closes what's open (a sheet, a menu, a window), otherwise goes back a page. In a box you're typing in, Backspace deletes as usual. |
| **Escape** | Closes what's open and puts the focus back where it was. |
| **Home** | The TV's own Home button belongs to the TV: it leaves the browser. Usherr's Home is the first place in the menu. |
| **Options** (the Options or Menu key, the context-menu key, or **M** on a keyboard) | Opens **More** for what has the focus: on a poster, that title's More; in the player, the controls; in a reader, its settings; in the guide, Remind me, Record, Start over and the channel's star. |
| **Play/Pause** | Plays and pauses: in the player, in the music player, and in Live TV. |
| **Fast forward and Rewind** | In the player, 30 seconds on or back. In music, the next or previous track. In a reader, the next or previous page. |
| **Microphone** | Voice search through the TV's own keyboard: move to a search box, press OK, then the keyboard's microphone. |
| **Channel up and down** (and Page Up, Page Down) | While a channel is on: the next or previous channel. **Digits** tune by number. |
| **Sliders** (volume, poster size) | Press **OK** to grab a slider; then Left and Right move it. **OK** or **Back** lets go, so the arrows never get stuck in it. |
| **Switches** on settings pages | Right turns one on, Left turns it off. |

Open windows and sheets keep the arrows inside them until they close. A
TV with no keyboard of its own gets a **keyboard on screen** in the search
box (the grid button): letters in a grid you move through with the arrows,
plus your recent searches.

### Screen by screen

- **Title pages** have one main button with the first focus: **Play** (on a
  show, *Play S1 E4*: the episode you were part way through, or the first
  one), or **Ask for it**. Then **Stack**, **Trailer** and **More**. More
  holds the rest: book a showtime, watch with a friend, mark watched, rate it,
  subtitles and audio, Show me how, and for administrators a better copy,
  choosing a release, the artwork and the files. Seasons and episodes go by
  the arrows; **More like this** is one row.
- **The player** has its own controls: the title, a bar that Left and Right
  move by 10 seconds (OK or Play/Pause pauses), then Subtitles, Audio,
  another release for a stream, and Start over. They fade while it plays and
  come back with any key; Back hides them, Back again leaves the film. Live
  TV, and the Neu and Classic looks, keep the browser's own controls.
- **Live TV** opens on a guide made for the remote: channels down the side,
  two hours across and a glowing line at now. Up and Down land on what's on
  at the same time on the next channel, Left and Right go along a channel. OK
  on what's on now watches it; OK on a later programme lets you set a
  reminder. While you watch, OK shows or hides the overlay (the channel,
  what's on, a progress line and Up Next), Up and Down change channel, and
  Back goes back to the guide.
- **Readers** (manga, comics, books, PDFs): Left and Right turn the page the
  way the book reads (Left goes on in a right-to-left manga), and so do Fast
  forward and Rewind. OK shows or hides the bar, Up moves to it, Back closes
  what's open and then the book. Options opens the reader settings as a list
  you change with Left and Right; in a manga it also has **Save page**, which used to need a long press or a right-click.
- **Music**: Play/Pause, and Fast forward and Rewind for the next or previous
  track. On an artist's page each album is one stop: OK plays it, Options
  shows the rest.
- **Movie night** shows tonight's showtime first (Open the lobby, Invite,
  Book another), your crew's vote as cards you press, and what's coming up.
  **The lobby** is a full screen: pick your seat with the arrows and OK, chat
  with quick replies, react, and Start, Skip the pre-show, use your phone or
  Leave.
- **The channel editor**: the name is the only typing; the number has − and +;
  shows, order and start times are buttons. The rest is under **Advanced**.
- **Server › Today** has three tiles: Downloads, Wanted and Needs you. In
  **Activity** each download is one stop: OK shows Pause, Retry, Find another,
  Stop or Remove (Remove always asks first).

## Sign in on a TV

Typing a password with a remote is slow, so a TV signs in with your phone.

1. On the TV's sign-in page, choose **Sign in with your phone**. A TV's own
   browser starts there by itself.
2. The TV shows a code like `ABCD-EFGH` and a QR code. Scan it with a phone
   that's already signed in to Usherr. (Or open **`/tv-code`** on the phone
   and type the code.)
3. Check the phone shows the same code and the kind of TV that asked, then
   press **Approve**. The TV signs in as you.

A code works once, for five minutes. The phone warns you when the TV isn't
on your home network; never approve a code someone sends you. The TV is
listed under **Signed-in devices** ("Chrome on NVIDIA Shield"), where you can
sign it out. An administrator can switch TV sign-in off under **Server ›
People › Ways to sign in** (in Classic or Neu: **Settings › Account & Access
› Ways to sign in**). How it's kept safe: [Sign in on a
TV](../security.md#sign-in-on-a-tv).

## Finish on your phone

Pages with typing to do (a review, a chat, a setting) offer **Finish on your
phone**. Settings pages have it at the top on a TV-size screen, and the lobby
has it for the chat and for using your phone as the remote. It shows a QR code and a short code; scanning it opens
the same page on your phone, signed in as you. Type there, then press
**Done** on the TV.

The link works once, for ten minutes, and only for you; it carries nothing
secret and signs nobody in. More in [Finish on your
phone](../security.md#finish-on-your-phone).

## Your settings

**You › Your settings** holds the settings that are yours alone. Its first
page, **Look and language**, shows them as rows you change with Left and
Right (the look and the language switch when you press OK):

- **The menu** (Always open, Icons, Hidden) and how fast it slides away with a
  mouse.
- **Look**: Broadcast, Usherr Neu, Neu with the new layout, or Usherr Classic.
- **Light or dark**. Broadcast is made dark first, for a TV in a dim room;
  there's a light version too.
- **Text size**: Regular or TV (everything bigger, for the sofa; a
  TV's own browser picks TV by itself).
- **Language** of the menus and buttons.

Then your profile, security, linked services, your MangaDex account, your
statistics, what you watched, and About. These are kept per browser where
they're about the screen (look, menu, text size) and per account otherwise.

## Server: the seven groups

Administrators find the server's settings under **Server**, in seven groups.
The pages themselves are the same as in Classic and Neu; only how they're
grouped and named changed.

1. **Setup and health**: Setup checklist, Service checks, Logs and errors,
   Server and updates, Backup and restore, Move from other apps.
2. **Libraries**: Media types, Folders, File names and importing, Quality,
   Automatic searching, Movie and show info (with Scores), Subtitles, Import
   lists, Artwork (Re-Posterr), Collections, Poster badges.
3. **Getting media**: Indexers, Usenet, Torrents, VPN and proxy, Download
   rules, Schedules and quiet times, Streaming.
4. **Watching**: Media servers (with Jellyfin or Emby and Converting), Live TV,
   Pre-show, Marquee, Play on TV, Apps on phones and TVs.
5. **Kinds of media**: Manga, Comics, Music, Books and Read-to-Me, Sports,
   NSFW 18+.
6. **People**: Accounts, Ways to sign in, Requests and reports, Linked
   Usherrs, Community, Notifications.
7. **Smarts and connections**: AI, News and in theaters, Other apps, Add-ons,
   Addresses.

## Switching back to Neu or Classic

Any time, per browser:

- In Broadcast: **You › Your settings › Look and language › Look**, then
  press OK on **Usherr Neu**, **Neu, new layout** or **Usherr Classic**.
- In Classic or Neu: **Settings › General › Light or dark**, the **Look**
  buttons.

Switching reloads the page. Your other settings, your library and everything
you've watched are the same in every look.
