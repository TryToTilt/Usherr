# Manga

Manga is its own section, apart from Comics. You add a series by its English title, Usherr looks for its volumes through your indexers, and it tells you in plain words what's happening and why nothing has come yet.

It's off until you switch it on in **Settings > Media types**.

## Reading

Tap a volume that's on disk to read it, or press **Continue** on the series' page or in the **Continue reading** row on Manga.

- **Direction:** manga reads right to left, and manhwa and manhua are one long scroll, top to bottom. A file's own ComicInfo.xml can say otherwise. Whatever you choose in the reader is remembered for this series, for you.
- **How pages show:** two pages side by side on a wide screen, one on a phone, or a long scroll. The cover is alone, and a two-page spread stays whole.
- **Turning:** tap the edge, swipe, or drag the page. Right to left, "next" lifts from the left edge. Arrow keys go the way the book reads, and Space always goes on.
- **Page turn:** Curl, Slide or None, in the reader's settings. It's the same choice as the book reader's, on each device. A device asking for less motion just turns.
- **Panels:** press **Panels** in the reader and the page glides from panel to panel, the same as in Comics, for chapters as well as volumes. Manga goes right to left, manhwa top to bottom, and your choice is kept for the series. **Whole page** shows the page, and the next tap goes on to the first panel. A picture of two pages is read one page, then the other. A page with no panels to find (a cover, a splash page) is shown whole, and on an upright phone a wide one is shown a page at a time. PDFs read by the page, because their pages are drawn in your browser.
- **Pairs of pages:** if a series' two-page pictures come out split across a turn, tick **Shift the pairs by one page** (it shows when two pages are on screen). It's kept for the series.
- **More for this device**, under the sliders button: **Fit** (whole page, width, height or actual size; a page too big for the screen scrolls), **Trim white borders**, **Brightness** and **Dark pages**, which turns white paper black. A curl only turns a whole page, so other fits slide instead.
- **Saving a page:** hold a finger on it, or right-click, then **Save page**.
- **The screen stays on** while you read, where the browser allows it (on a phone that usually needs an https address), until 15 minutes pass with no turn.
- **The last page** offers the next volume, or says it isn't here yet.
- **Your place** is kept per person, on any device. Reaching the last page marks a volume read, and the next one takes its place in Continue. Under **⋯** on a volume: mark read, mark read up to here, save the file, and **Keep on this device** to read it with no connection.

CBZ and CBR read as they are. A PDF's pages are drawn in your browser, a few at a time (not the long scroll, and not kept on a device). An EPUB made of pictures reads like any other, in the book's own order.

## Discover

**Manga > Discover** shows what's trending, popular or new on AniList, and you can pick a genre. It needs no key or account, and only accounts allowed to add manga see it. 18+ series and novels aren't on these shelves.

**Add** on a tile is one press. **Add gets** above the tiles says what that press wants: all volumes, new volumes only or nothing yet, remembered on this device. A series with no official English edition known waits, and its page says why and offers fan scans. Tap a cover for more about it first.

**From an AniList list** takes a user name and shows what that person is reading and plans to read. The list has to be public. There's no sign-in, and nothing on AniList changes.

A series' page shows its genres and score, then **Related series** and **More like this**, from AniList. They're read when you open the page and not stored, so a series added by name, or AniList being away, just goes without them.

## Following new volumes

A series that follows new volumes (All volumes or New volumes only) tells you when it gets one: an entry on the calendar for the day it was noticed, and a notification. Notifications you set up by hand need **New manga volumes** ticked in **Settings > Notifications**. An 18+ series isn't announced either way.

AniList has no dates for volumes, and often no volume count until a series ends. So a volume is noticed when AniList's count goes up, or when your indexers have a confident release of one past the last you know of, and the calendar's date is that day, not its date in shops.

Each series' details are refreshed between 2 and 9 in the morning: running series about daily, finished ones monthly.

Read progress isn't sent to AniList. That may come later.

## Add a series

Press **Add a series** and type the title. English works, and so do the Japanese or Korean title and other names, because the titles come from [AniList](https://anilist.co). Nothing is saved until you press **Add**.

Then say what Usherr should get:

| Choice | What happens |
|---|---|
| **All volumes** | Every volume is wanted. Usherr looks once by the series' name right away, and the automatic search does the rest. |
| **New volumes only** | Volumes that exist now aren't wanted. A new one is, when it turns up. |
| **Nothing yet** | Usherr doesn't look. Mark the volumes you want on the series' page. |

When a search finds nothing, the page says why: a spelling AniList doesn't know, AniList being away, or results left out because they're novels or 18+ and your account isn't allowed them. You can still add a series by the name you typed, without AniList.

AniList's volume count is often the Japanese count, and it's empty for running series. Usherr adds any volume a confident release names, and **New volume** on the series' page adds one by hand.

## Where manga comes from

Set this once in **Settings > Manga**. A single series can change it under **Sources for this series**.

| Source | Normally | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| **Your own files** | Always on | Files in your manga folder |
| **Official English editions** | On | The published English translation, recognised by its release name on your indexers |
| **Fan scans from your indexers** | Off | Volumes translated by fans. Only used when no official edition turns up, or for series with none |
| **Fan scans from MangaDex** | Off | Chapters translated by fans, for series you choose to follow by chapter. See [Chapters from MangaDex](#chapters-from-mangadex) |
| **Nyaa** | Off | A public torrent index. Its results are sorted like any other |
| **Scraped sites** | Not built | Usherr doesn't have these |

**Sources for this series** has three choices. *My settings* follows this page. *Official only* takes no fan scans, even if they're on here. *Also fan scans* allows them for this series alone.

### Nyaa

Turning the switch on asks first, because torrents upload to other people while they seed, and that is the part rights holders tend to pursue. Then Usherr adds Nyaa to your indexers from its list of sites. Nyaa itself isn't asked until a search needs it.

- It's for Manga only. On the indexer page it shows as switched off, which is why films and TV never ask it. Switching it on there would let them.
- It asks for English translations. Language and series are still checked by name, and a torrent nobody is sharing is turned down.
- **Your VPN settings apply.** With torrents required to use the VPN, Nyaa's downloads wait for it. If your indexers go through the VPN, so do its searches. With a debrid service or your own download client, torrents go there, and that decides what's shared.
- If you added a Nyaa yourself, Usherr uses it and changes nothing about it.
- It can't be turned on while torrents are switched off. Turning it off again stops the searches and leaves the indexer in place.

**"Official" describes the edition, not the copy.** No service lets a program download official English manga as plain files, so Usherr goes by the release's name. A copy on an indexer still isn't a purchase. Every series with a known publisher has a **Buy it** link.

### How a release is judged

- **Official**: a volume-shaped name with a publisher's name, words like `Retail`, `HYBRID.MANGA` or `Hybrid.Comic`, a group known for releasing the published editions, or `(Digital)` on a series with a known English publisher. The publisher often comes first, as in `VIZ.Media.Chainsaw.Man.Vol.21.2026.HYBRID.MANGA.eBook-21A1`, and the series is read after it.
- **Fan scan**: words such as `scanlation` or `scans`, known fan groups, and anything named as a chapter. A whole run of chapters, such as `(Chapters 1-721)`, counts as fan scans. It's turned down unless the series follows chapters and fan scans from your indexers are on. Then it waits for you, and never downloads by itself.
- **Don't know**: anything else. These never download by themselves. They wait for you under **Waiting for you**, with the reason, whichever sources are on.

A sure volume of a source you allow downloads by itself. These are turned down, and counted under **Why nothing yet?**:

- another language (Japanese or raw, Korean, Chinese, French `T25`, and others)
- chapters (`c123`, `ch.123`, `Chapter 123`) from your indexers, as Usherr searches them for volumes
- other series and spin-offs. The series' own name is read first, so `Mob Psycho 100 v01` and `Kaiju No. 8 v01` are volume 1 and not number 100 or 8, and other titles count too
- videos, novels, MOBI and AZW3
- an EPUB with no volume number

Packs (`v01-v50`, or a whole series), bare numbers (`Name 023`, which may be a chapter) and a volume named as an EPUB, which may be the novel, wait for you. A pack fills every volume it holds with one download.

### What a download turns out to hold

Nobody can tell what an eBook release holds until it's here, so Usherr looks when it arrives. CBZ, CBR, PDF and an EPUB of pictures are filed. If it holds only other formats, or an EPUB that's mostly words, Activity says what it found. That release isn't blocklisted, since nothing's wrong with it, but it won't download again without you choosing it.

The list of group names is a starting point. If real releases on your indexers land on the wrong side, the names of those releases are what's needed to tune it.

## When Usherr looks

Automatic searches run with the others, and stop at each indexer's daily limit. Your limits and grab limits apply: at most ten downloads an hour and ten in the line at once.

1. **First by name, once.** One search by the series' title says if your indexers carry it at all. It also finds its volumes and packs. Other titles are only tried if the title found nothing.
2. **Then by volume, only if the name found something.** Up to ten searches a run in all, with the words `Vol 23`, `v023`, `v23`, `Volume 23`, stopping at the first that finds it. Searches ask your indexers' Books categories, as published manga is filed under Books, Books > EBook and Books > Comics.
3. **Nothing found? It waits longer.** A day, three days, a week, then a month. The page says when the next look is. **Search now**, a new volume or a change of sources makes it look sooner.

An indexer that's out of searches for the day isn't asked, and that doesn't count against the series.

## Your own files

Point the manga folder at your files in **Settings > Library > Folders**, or set `MANGA_DIR`. Without either, it uses `/manga` if that's mounted, else `/data/manga`.

**Look through the folder now** in **Settings > Manga** finds files named like `Series/Series v01.cbz`, or `Series v01.cbz` loose in the folder, and marks those volumes as on disk where they are. Nothing is moved, renamed or deleted, and your files aren't changed. A series that isn't in your library is listed with a button to add it. It also runs once a night, between 2 and 9 in the morning, and when you add a series.

A file that's gone is forgotten, and that volume stops being wanted, so a drive that's away for an hour can't start a round of downloads. A folder that can't be read, or has nothing in it, changes nothing.

## What Usherr files

Volumes go in `Series/Series v01.cbz`, and `v12.5` for a half volume. Komga and Kavita can read this folder as it is. CBZ files Usherr downloads get a `ComicInfo.xml` with the series, the number, and that it reads right to left. Files that already have one keep it.

**A volume is never filed twice.** The same file is dropped. Another copy goes under **Waiting for you**, where **Take it** swaps it in and moves your earlier file to the folder's `.replaced`, and **Keep mine** leaves things as they are. A copy from a pack downloads the pack again and swaps only that volume.

When a pack holds a volume twice, the better copy is filed: an official edition over a fan scan, then a CBZ or CBR over an EPUB over a PDF, then the bigger file. Activity counts the others.

## Chapters from MangaDex

[MangaDex](https://mangadex.org) hosts chapters translated by fans. **It's off until you switch it on** in **Settings > Manga**, and with it off Usherr never asks it anything. Official editions stay the default: a series only uses MangaDex if you choose to follow its chapters. No MangaDex account is needed, and Usherr doesn't use one.

The **Chapters** tab on a series appears once the switch is on. Choose **New ones** or **All of them** there, or switch on **Also follow new chapters** when adding a series. **Official only** under *Sources for this series* keeps MangaDex away from that series.

**Finding the entry.** Usherr uses the link between AniList and MangaDex when either has one. Otherwise, one exact title in the right years is enough. Anything less asks you: the tab lists the likely ones, or you can paste its MangaDex address. A doujinshi or an entry linked to another series is never picked.

**What a chapter can say:**

| Pill | What it means |
|---|---|
| **On disk** | Here. Tap to read. |
| **Downloading** | In a batch. See Activity. |
| **Wanted** / **Not wanted** | Tap for **Download** and **Want it**. |
| **In Volume N** | A volume you have holds this chapter. Tap to read that volume. |
| **On another site** | Hosted elsewhere, usually by the publisher. It can't be downloaded, so it's skipped. Tap to read it there. |
| **Taken down** | Listed on MangaDex with its pages removed. |
| **Trying again later** | Its pages couldn't be fetched just now. It tries again after an hour, then less often. |

**Why nothing yet?** A series can look fine on MangaDex and have nothing to download. Usherr counts what's listed, what's taken down and what's only a link, and says so. If it's licensed in English, that's most likely why, and official volumes are the place to look. A series with nothing waits longer each time: a day, three days, a week, then a month.

**When several groups did a chapter**, Usherr takes the one you chose for this series. Without a choice, it takes the group that did the most chapters, so the translation stays in one voice, then the fullest, then the first published. A chapter you have keeps its group, even if you choose another later. Nothing is fetched twice.

**The files** go in `Series/Chapters/Series c0123 [Group].cbz`, apart from your volumes, so Komga and Kavita don't mix them in by default. Each has a `ComicInfo.xml` with the series, the number, the volume, the group as translator, and MangaDex as the source. A file already in that folder with a chapter's number is counted as that chapter.

**Reading.** The last page of a chapter offers the next chapter or volume, whichever comes next. Your place and Continue work across both. Bookmarks aren't on chapters yet, and chapters can't be kept on a device yet.

**When a volume arrives** from another source, its chapters show **In Volume N** and aren't downloaded any more. Your place comes along: anyone who read all of its chapters has read the volume, and anyone part way through opens at about the same page. It's counted in pages, so a cover or extras can put it a little off. **Tidy chapters** on the tab moves those chapter files to the folder's `.replaced`, and **Put chapters back** returns them. They're never deleted. **Tidy chapters once their volume arrives** in **Settings > Manga** does it by itself.

**A run of chapters from your indexers.** If a series follows chapters and fan scans from your indexers are on, a release like `One Piece (Chapters 1-721)` waits for you under **Packs**. If its files are one chapter each, they're filed as chapters, and any you already have are left alone. If it's one big file, Usherr can't split it, so nothing is filed and Activity says why.

### How gently it asks

- **Looking:** a few series every 15 minutes, each one every four hours or so, and every day once a series is finished. **Look now** asks right away, at most once a minute.
- **Downloading:** one batch at a time, one page at a time. A batch is one line in Activity, with Pause, Stop and Retry. It uses the download line's rules for when it may start, then gives its place back, so it can't keep a film waiting. It doesn't use your Usenet connections.
- **Catching up:** by day a series gets a batch only if ten chapters or fewer are waiting. Bigger catch-ups run between 2 and 9 in the morning, up to 40 chapters at a time. **Get the next 10 now** is there if you can't wait.
- **A restart** carries on with the pages already fetched.
- **When MangaDex is away** or asks Usherr to slow down, nothing is asked until the time is up, even after a restart. The tab and Activity say so. An outage waits 15 minutes, then longer each time, up to six hours.
- **Language and quality:** English by default, with others in **Settings > Manga**. **Data saver** takes smaller pages of lower quality.

### MangaDex's rules

Usherr follows [its API rules](https://api.mangadex.org/docs/): about five requests a second at most, and 40 a minute for page addresses (it stays under both). It sends its own name, not a browser's, tells MangaDex's page servers how each image went, and asks for a new address when a page fails. Its [acceptable use policy](https://api.mangadex.org/docs/) asks that MangaDex and scanlation groups are credited, that groups' removal requests are honoured, and that nobody charges for access or runs ads on it. Usherr credits both in the page and in every file, and has no ads. **That's about your use too:** this is for your own household, so don't sell access to a server that uses it.

Files you already have stay when a chapter is later taken down. The chapter says MangaDex no longer has it, and what you do with your copy is up to you.

**"Fan scan" means it isn't licensed.** Most of what's on MangaDex is unlicensed. If a series has an English publisher, consider buying it.

## Bring manga over from Comics

**Settings > Manga** lists comic series Comics knows as manga, by the kind you gave them or their publisher. Nothing happens until you press a button, and it's never done for you.

1. **Look first.** Choose which series it is on AniList, or keep its name.
2. **The preview** shows every file's new name, and which stay and why. Nothing has changed yet.
3. **Move** moves the files into the manga folder and makes the volumes. A comic's number becomes its volume. Where people had got to, their bookmarks, what they've read and how they read it come too.
4. **Undo** puts the files, reading places and the comic series back as they were. A file changed since, or whose old name has been taken, stays and is listed.

Files are moved and never deleted or overwritten:

- An **identical copy** stays where it is, for you to remove if you like.
- **Another copy** of a volume stays where it is.
- A file whose **new name is taken**, or that Manga already has, stays where it is. Its series then stays in Comics too.
- A name that **doesn't say which volume** stays where it is.

A comic series with **no files** comes over with one tick, keeping what it wanted. If AniList has exactly that title it brings the details, and otherwise the series keeps its name. A series that looks like a magazine is marked, and best left in Comics.

The moved series is hidden from Comics, not deleted, which is what makes Undo possible. Adding it to Comics again brings it back.

## Reading apps

Manga is in the **OPDS** catalogue next to Comics, so Panels, Chunky and KOReader can read a page at a time, or download the file, and show your place. A PDF is offered for download only. Manga isn't in OPDS search yet, and Subsonic and Kobo aren't for pictures.

## Who can do what

- **Everyone** can browse Manga and save files. 18+ series only show for accounts allowed 18+ content.
- **Accounts with the Manga permission** (on by default, off for view-only accounts) can add series.
- **Administrators** can search, choose releases, change sources, look through the folder and remove series. Removing a series leaves its files where they are.

## Data from MangaDex

Only with its switch on, and only for series you follow by chapter or ask about on the tab, Usherr asks MangaDex for that series' entry and chapters, and stores what's on the tab. It sends nothing about you beyond the usual address and `Usherr/<version>`.

## Data from AniList

Usherr asks AniList for titles, one request a second at most, and keeps an answer in memory for a while: six hours for a search or a Discover shelf, a day for a series' genres and related series, 15 minutes for someone's list. Only series you add are stored, and they're refreshed at night. AniList doesn't need a key, and with no series added it isn't asked until you search or open Discover.
