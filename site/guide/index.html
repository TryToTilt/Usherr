Show me how

Everything you can do with Usherr, step by step. In the app, the same walkthroughs light up the real buttons for you.

Watch

  • Find something for tonightThree picks from what you already have: what you’re part-way through, the next episode, or something unseen.
  • Let Usherr pick (Roulette)Can’t decide? Spin for one title, from what you have or something new.
  • Ask for something in your own wordsThe Ask bar: “a 90s heist movie, not too violent”, and Usherr finds real titles.
  • Browse DiscoverThe rows, the filters, Hide what I have and the family filter.
  • Watch a film now, or keep itStream plays it straight away without downloading; Add to library downloads it to keep.
  • Get a TV showThe whole show, only new episodes, or just the ones you pick.
  • Follow an actor or directorSee their new and coming films and shows as soon as they’re announced.
  • See what’s comingNew episodes and films on the way, what Usherr is still looking for, and the people you follow.
  • Teach Usherr your tasteThumbs up and down make your For you suggestions better. Rate 20 is the quick start.
  • Keep a list of what to watch nextYour stack: the films and shows you’ve pulled out to watch, as a stack of tickets that follows you to your phone.
  • Your diary and your year (Wrapped)What you watched and when, with stars and notes, and your year in watching.

Movie night

  • Book a showtimeA film at a set time, with trailers and a pre-show first, like at the cinema.
  • Set up a pre-show for movie nightAdministratorsTrailers, your own clips and the cinema look before the film. Ready as it is; make it yours.
  • Make a Marquee screenAdministratorsA lobby board for a tablet or a screen by the TV: what’s playing, coming soon, trailers, your slides.
  • Steer the Marquee from your phoneAdministratorsThe phone remote: next, pause, pin, dim, blank, info and the pre-show, for every screen.
  • Watch Live TVChannels like broadcast TV, made from your library and more: the guide, channel up and down, Multiview.
  • Make a Live TV channelAdministratorsA sitcom channel, Saturday-morning cartoons, a film night: from your library, streamed shows or a free live stream.
  • Follow a sports teamYour team’s games, listed and ready to stream or download, with the scores hidden.

Listen

  • Play some musicPick an artist, press Play, and it keeps playing while you move around Usherr.
  • Get music by an artistAdd an artist and choose which albums Usherr gets: all of them, only new ones, or ones you pick.
  • Make a playlistYour own list of tracks, private or shared with the household.
  • Listen to an audiobookUsherr Books: your audiobooks, with chapters, speed, a sleep timer, and your place kept on every device.
  • Turn an ebook into an audiobookAdministratorsRead-to-Me reads a book aloud with a computer voice and saves it as an audiobook.

Read

  • Get a bookFind an ebook (or audiobook) by title or author and Usherr gets it.
  • Read an ebookUsherr Books: a reader with your text size and colours, and your place kept on every device. Or send books to an e-reader.
  • Read a comicFind a series, open an issue, and read it panel by panel or turning the pages.
  • Read a big story in the right orderStory arcs: one story told across several series (Civil War, Knightfall), as a reading list in order.
  • See this week’s new comicsThis week: every comic out this week, and how to get the ones you want.
  • Check comic downloads Usherr wasn’t sure aboutAdministratorsThe Review tab: releases that might be the wrong issue wait there for you to say yes or no.

People and devices

  • Add a family member, kids tooAdministratorsTheir own account, with their own taste, stack and diary, and only the rights you give them.
  • Let the kids ask instead of addAdministratorsTheir Add button becomes Request; you approve or decline.
  • Watch on your phone (Usherr as an app)Open Usherr on a phone and keep it on the home screen, like an app.
  • Usherr in Stremio or NuvioOne add-on link puts your library, streams, watchlist and Live TV in the Stremio and Nuvio apps.
  • Get notices on your phoneAdministratorsA message when a film arrives, a request comes in, an audiobook is ready, or a show you set a reminder for starts.

My library

  • Complete a film seriesAdministratorsAny trilogy or franchise: see what you have, what’s missing and what’s coming, and get the rest in one go.
  • Fill the gaps in your libraryComplete your library: what’s missing from series, directors and actors you collect, and the classics.
  • Swap a film for a better copyA better quality, another edition, or a smaller file: pick the release yourself.
  • Get subtitlesSubtitles in your language for a film, and how to have them come by themselves.
  • Make a Kids shelf (tags)Tag films and shows, and each tag becomes a collection in Jellyfin: a Kids shelf, a Christmas one.
  • Move a film to another folderAdministratorsKeep cartoons, documentaries or 4K copies in their own folder, files and all.
  • Why didn’t my download work?Where Usherr says what went wrong, in plain words, and what to try.
  • Restyle a posterAdministratorsPick the poster, background and logo Jellyfin shows, or give them your own style.
  • Bring in the music you already haveAdministratorsUsherr finds your existing collection where it is: nothing is moved or changed.

Setup

  • Set up comicsAdministratorsSwitch comics on with a free ComicVine key, so Usherr can find series, new issues and story arcs.
  • Bring in the films and shows you already haveAdministratorsUsherr matches your folders to the right titles; nothing is moved, renamed or downloaded again.
  • Set up Play on TVAdministratorsSend what you’re watching to an Android TV box (an NVIDIA Shield), with one tap in the player.
  • Choose what Usherr handlesAdministratorsFilms, shows, anime, music, books, comics, sports: switch on the kinds you want.
  • Add a Usenet providerAdministratorsThe service your downloads come from: what it is, how to choose one, and where it goes.
  • Add an indexerAdministratorsThe search engine for Usenet: what it is, where its key comes from, and Prowlarr if you use it.
  • Choose the qualityAdministrators1080p or 4K: what each costs in disk space, and what Usherr looks for.
  • Keep the TV smooth while downloadingAdministratorsSchedules: slow downloads while the family watches, and do the heavy work at night.
  • Set up a VPN for torrentsAdministratorsKeep torrent traffic private: what a VPN does here, and how to add yours.
  • Backups and updatesAdministratorsKeep a copy of your settings and library list, and keep Usherr up to date.
  • Move over from Radarr, Sonarr or ProwlarrAdministratorsBring your films, shows, indexers and settings from the apps you use now; nothing is deleted there.
  • Connect JellyfinAdministratorsSo new films show up in Jellyfin at once, Play in Jellyfin buttons appear, and your Diary fills itself.
  • Set up Plex with the virtual driveAdministratorsStreamed films and shows in Plex: Usherr serves them as a drive of ordinary video files, and rclone puts it on your computer.
  • Download clientsAdministratorsUsherr downloads by itself; connect SABnzbd, qBittorrent or a debrid account only if you want to.
  • Choose how files are namedAdministratorsHow films and episodes are named and filed, to suit Jellyfin, Plex or your own taste.
  • Follow a list from Trakt, IMDb or LetterboxdAdministratorsA list somewhere else, read on a schedule: whatever is new on it comes into your library.
