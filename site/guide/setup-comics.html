Set up comicsAdministrators

Switch comics on with a free ComicVine key, so Usherr can find series, new issues and story arcs.

  1. What you need

    Usherr gets every series, issue, cover and story arc from ComicVine, a free comics database. It needs a key: a code that says the requests come from you. It takes a couple of minutes and costs nothing.

  2. Get your ComicVine key

    Open comicvine.gamespot.com/api in a new tab, sign in (or make a free account), and your key is shown on that page. Copy it, then come back here.

  3. Paste your key

    Usherr checks it with ComicVine before saving it.

  4. Comics are on

    Your comics live here. The Add tab finds a series to follow; new issues then come in by themselves. Files go in the comics folder shown in the settings tab (the sliders).

  5. Ready to read

    Add a series, or use This week to see what’s new.

Made from Usherr’s own Show Me How walkthroughs. In the app, Show me how in the top bar walks you through each of these on the real screens.
